Denver · ambassador badge

A badge people would actually wear

Six directions for the Denver Ambassador badge, each drawn from a Denver thing (the columbine, the Rockies, 300 sunny days, the bison, 5280) and tested where it has to live: a sticker, an enamel pin, a tote, and the tiny badge next to a name in the app.

Original drawings, nothing taken from a team or city logo. Type is Fraunces, rounder and warmer than the serif we dropped. Colors are Benable purple plus sky, sun and pine.

Why a flower or a mascot, not a sealPeople buy and wear things that are cute on their own. A seal says “official”; a columbine pin says “I love Denver”, and it also happens to say Benable.
It has to work tinyThe in-app badge is 16 to 22 px next to a name. Directions with one simple shape (columbine, sun, stamp) survive; detailed scenes don’t.
Room to growPick a system, not a one-off: a flower per city, a stamp per city, or a 2026 edition, so ambassadors collect them and new cities get their own.

The Columbine

Colorado’s state flower, which happens to be Benable purple. A flower pin, not a logo, so people wear it for itself.

Enamel pin, embroidered patch, a sticker on a laptop. Reads at 16 px as a purple star.
