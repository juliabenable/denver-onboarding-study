Going small first

What could start with no engineering at all

  • Dec 11One Instagram group chat with the ambassadors. Olivia turns good answers into recs.
  • WeeklyA Friday text to answerers: “3 people went to your pick.”
  • WeeklyA prompt on ambassadors’ Stories, with a Sunday roundup.
  • WeeklyA this-or-that Story poll. Count votes and replies.
  • OnceCompare the ambassadors’ Top 5s by hand and tell people who they overlap with.
  • Two weeks inCheck: are half the asks coming without a nudge, and is the first answer inside an hour?

If the group chat works by hand, it tells us what to build. If it doesn’t, we’ve lost two weeks, not a sprint.
